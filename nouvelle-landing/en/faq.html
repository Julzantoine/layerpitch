Frequently asked questions

General

What is LayerPitch?

A workspace to compose, present, test and validate the music and sound design of a video game, between composers, sound designers and studios.

What is an AdReel?

An "Adaptive Reel": a presentation of interactive music (state changes, branching, intensity changes, random pools) that you share with a simple link.

Do I need to install anything?

No. An AdReel, a showcase or a project opens like a web page.

Do I need an account to listen?

No. Listening to AdReels, packs and showcases is always free, with no account.

Access

How do I get access to LayerPitch?

LayerPitch is currently in closed beta, by invitation, for composers, sound designers and studios. Leave your email: the invitation arrives within the day and stays valid for 24 hours.

What happens at the public launch?

All beta testers keep their account and get a discount as a thank-you.

Composers and sound designers

What does the studio see when I send an AdReel?

They play with the music: they adjust the intensity, mute layers, explore the branches. On your side, you see who opened the link, which layers were tested and how long they stayed.

What adaptive playback modes are there?

Five: loopable static, vertical layering, randomized layering, branching sequential and random sequential.

Can I sell my music?

Yes, through packs, with a Stripe Connect payout and a billing mandate. Real sales will only start at the public launch.

Studios

What are the Moodboard and the Project space?

A shared space to gather references, images, videos, notes and tracks, build the sound direction, talk with your composer or sound designer and test versions on video.

Who can see my project?

Only the members you invite. Files are private and read through temporary links. Showcases are public or secret-link, as you choose.

Can I invite my composer and my team?

Yes. The number of members depends on your tier.

Pricing and data

How much does it cost?

There is always a free tier, then you scale up when you need to. Composers: Rookie (free), Warrior, Boss. Studios: SoloDev, Indie, AA, AAA. Details on the Pricing page. If you are both a composer and a studio, with a paid studio tier your composer subscription is half price.

Who owns the music I put on LayerPitch?

It stays yours. LayerPitch only hosts and showcases it.

Is my data safe?

Your database and accounts are hosted in Europe (Ireland), with Supabase. Your files are stored separately, with Cloudflare, with a Western Europe location. Project files are private and can only be read through temporary links, and access rights are checked server-side on every request.

Can I leave LayerPitch and delete my data?

Yes. If you delete your account, all your data is erased, except the music you have sold to third parties.
