LayerPitch vs Wwise and FMOD

They are not competitors: they come in at different moments. Wwise and FMOD are audio engines (middleware) you integrate into a game to play its music and sounds. LayerPitch comes before: presenting, listening to, testing on video and validating adaptive music between composer and studio, from a simple web link, with nothing to install.

When should I use LayerPitch?

When the composer needs to show how the music reacts, and the studio needs to try it, compare it and validate it before committing. The studio plays with the intensity, mutes layers, explores the branches and tests versions on its game video, with nothing to install. The composer sees who opened the link, which layers were tested and for how long.

When should I use Wwise or FMOD?

When the music is validated and has to play inside the game itself: wiring game states to the music, mixing, optimising, integrating into the engine. LayerPitch does not do this work and does not replace your middleware.

How the two work together

The composer builds and presents their AdReel on LayerPitch; the studio validates the musical direction; the audio team then implements the chosen music in Wwise or FMOD. LayerPitch cuts the back-and-forth before integration, where it costs the least.

Questions about the comparison

Does LayerPitch replace Wwise or FMOD?

No. Wwise and FMOD play audio inside the game; LayerPitch is for presenting, testing and validating adaptive music before that integration.

Do I need to know Wwise or FMOD to use LayerPitch?

No. The composer builds their AdReel without writing code, and the studio listens in the browser.

Can I use LayerPitch together with Wwise or FMOD?

Yes, they complement each other: LayerPitch to present and validate the music, Wwise or FMOD to integrate it into the game afterwards.

Is LayerPitch free, like the indie versions of Wwise and FMOD?

There is always a free tier (Rookie for composers, SoloDev for studios), and listening to an AdReel never needs an account. The free offers of Wwise and FMOD depend on the project budget: see their sites.
